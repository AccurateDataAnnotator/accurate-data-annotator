Cookie Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains how accuratedataannotator.com uses cookies and similar technologies such as your browser's local storage. You can accept or reject non-essential cookies in the banner, and change your choice at any time using the "Cookie settings" link in the footer. If you reject, the site still works normally.

1. Essential storage (always on)

These items make the platform work and do not need consent. They are stored in your own browser and are not used for advertising.

ItemPurposeDuration
consent_ada_v1Remembers your cookie choiceUntil you clear your browser data or change your choice
Keys starting with "ada_"Saves your progress through the program, test answers and unlock status on your deviceUntil you clear your browser data or reset your device progress

2. Analytics (only if you accept)

We may use Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit the site and which pages are useful. It is loaded only after you accept analytics cookies. It may set cookies such as _ga and _ga_* that last up to two years.

3. Advertising (only if you accept)

We plan to show a small number of fixed, labelled advertisements on some pages using Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show and measure ads. If you reject advertising cookies, we do not show ad slots to you. For more information, see how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

To let Google verify and review our site, the AdSense code is included in our pages. When that code loads, Google may process limited technical data such as your IP address.

4. Third-party services that load on every page

To display the site correctly we load fonts from Google Fonts and icons from Font Awesome. Your browser contacts these providers directly, so they receive your IP address and browser details. Pages that use Google Forms, Google Drive or PayPal send you to those services, which have their own cookie policies.

5. How to control cookies

  • Use "Cookie settings" in the footer of any page to accept or reject again.
  • Clear or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Clearing local storage will also remove your saved progress on this device.
  • Visit Google Ad Settings to manage personalised ads.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: info@accuratedataannotator.com. See also our Privacy Policy.

About ADA

Accurate Data Annotator (ADA) is a bilingual Arabic-English data annotation company based in Cairo, Egypt. We prepare clean, accurately labelled data for AI and machine learning teams, and we train and certify the people who do that work.

What we do

  • Data annotation in English and Arabic: text, image, audio and video, checked through a multi-stage QA process before delivery.
  • LLM evaluation and alignment: structured review of AI model responses for accuracy, safety and clarity.
  • Data collection projects: gathering original, consented data from contributors, such as our Pet Image Collection.
  • The ADA Certification Program: practical training and assessment for people who want to work in AI data annotation.

Who is behind ADA

ADA was founded by Batoul H. Hassaballa, Founder and CEO, who brings more than 15 years of experience working with international organizations. She leads ADA's client work, training and quality standards.

Our standards

  • Accuracy first: guidelines, double-pass QA and an accuracy report for every dataset.
  • Honest claims: we only describe work we have actually done.
  • Respect for contributors and data: clear consent, clear rules and clear communication.
